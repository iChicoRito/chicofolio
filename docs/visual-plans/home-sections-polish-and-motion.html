Home sections: About, Projects, Tech Stack, Contact

One shared section header, gentle reveal-on-scroll, a cursor spotlight on project cards, and clearer layouts for the About and Contact sections.

Why

The four home sections are static text blocks with plain headings and no motion. The About section is only one paragraph and a button. In the Contact section, the social links sit in a separate card under the form, which feels disconnected.

Motion rules:

  • Keep motion small and purposeful.
  • Play each animation once.
  • Turn motion off when the visitor has turned it off on their device.
  • Never hide content if JavaScript fails to load.

Changes

All four sectionsNew shared SectionHeader: a styled "01 — About me" label, a SplitText heading, and intro text. Content fades and rises in once as it scrolls into view.
AboutTwo columns: the bio with skill chips and a "More about me" link, plus a recent experience list from profile.experience that highlights on hover.
ProjectsCards appear one after another. A soft cursor spotlight on project cards. The tab content fades in when you switch tabs. The Design and UI/UX cards lift and zoom like the project cards.
Tech StackGroups appear one after another. Tech tiles lift and their icon grows on hover.
ContactTwo columns: the pitch and social links (the arrow slides on hover) on the left, the form in a soft panel on the right.
