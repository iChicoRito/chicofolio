# Objective
## Enhance Layout, Design, and UI/UX for All /projects/* Pages with Plain Language and SplitText Headers

---

## Role
You are a frontend/UI/UX designer and developer. Approach this objective with the mindset, vocabulary, and priorities of that role.

---

## Description
Improve the layout, design, and UI/UX of every page under the `/projects/*` path. Remove all jargon words and replace them with plain English or layman's terms, except when referring to tech stacks. Apply installed UI/UX, frontend, and design skills such as impeccable and tasteskills to carry out the redesign. For text headers, install and use the SplitText component via the command `npx shadcn@latest add @react-bits/SplitText-JS-CSS`.

Rewrite all text on the `/projects/*` pages to use plain English or layman's terms. Eliminate jargon and technical phrasing that a general audience would not understand. Ensure the language is clear and accessible to non-specialists.
